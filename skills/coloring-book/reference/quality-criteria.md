# Coloring page quality criteria

A final page is accepted only if ALL of these hold.

1. **Line art only.** Pure black lines on a pure white background. No gray tones,
   color, fills, gradients or hatching.
2. **Colorable.** Most regions are closed shapes a child can fill in. No large open
   gaps in outlines.
3. **Fits the difficulty profile** (`difficulty-profiles.md`): line thickness and number
   of regions are in range.
4. **Right subject.** The subject is instantly recognizable and matches the page spec.
5. **Clean.** No text, watermark, logo, signature, URL, frame or page border.
   The subject is not cut off by the image edge.
6. **Safe for children.** Nothing violent, scary, sexual, hateful or otherwise
   inappropriate for ages 3–12.
7. **Book consistency.** In `variants` mode pages are clearly different pictures;
   in `set` mode pages share a similar style and difficulty.

Scoring guide (0–10): 9–10 print as is; 7–8 good, minor flaws; 5–6 usable only as a last
resort; ≤ 4 reject.
